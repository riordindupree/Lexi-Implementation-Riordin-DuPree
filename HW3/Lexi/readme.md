
### Developement Process

I ended up working on this project in three distinct parts, as was suggested when the assignment was first given to us. These parts were First, getting a window to appear from SwingWindow.java, Second, creating the Glyph character tree based on the Composite(163) design pattern, and Third, creating the layout system for the glyphs based on the Strategy(315) design pattern.

The first part of the assignment was not all that complicated, nor was it meant to be. The biggest issue I had was related to two simple and easily fixed misunderstandings. The first misunderstanding came from not understanding how to create the window. This was essentially a non-issue though, since the program just creates the window when SwingWindow is called. The second issue came from the fact that I simply had not known that the structuring of the program had to be started before I could get the window to appear, which was again an easy fix.

The second part of the assignment, the "actual first part," was also relatively simple. This was very much thanks to the examples gone over in class, and the requirement to create a UML for the structure of the program. By creating this document before getting started on the assignment, I had a good enough of an idea to figure out basically everything that I needed to do implementation wise. The in class examples, as well as the examples in the Github, were able to help me fairly easily figure how to structure the arrays in Row and Column. In fact, the biggest issue I faced during the project was actually just forgetting to look at that documentation, which required a brief, but simple redesign.

The final section of the assingment was much harder to complete. However, this was mainly due to several misunderstandings I had regarding the material, and the resources that helped me with the previous half of the assignment proved to be just as, if not, more helpful than with the previous section. I made sure to talk to the instructor as much as I could to make sure I got a good idea on my head regarding the assignment, which also helped out a lot. I had to rewrite several parts of this homework many times over to get it to a point at which I was happy with it, including having to redo the entirety of Column and Row due so that I could add the new CompositeGlyph feature. On top of this, there were also several methods I added to Glyph and its subclasses to help out with the Compositor functions. Overall, I did end up probably rewriting a bit more than I needed to, but the process of figuring out was still a lot of fun at the end of the day! Not to mention, due to how often I ended up rewriting it, I ended up not having that many bugs by the time I got to testing!
